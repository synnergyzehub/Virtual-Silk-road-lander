## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I get started implementing the strategic plan?
**A:** Start by reading STRATEGIC_PLANNING_SUMMARY.md, then use templates from SUB_ISSUE_TEMPLATES.md to create actionable GitHub issues for your area.

### Q: What's the expected timeline to profitability?
**A:** Break-even is projected at Month 22, with 10% operating margin by Year 2 and 20% by Year 3.

### Q: How much will AI automation actually reduce costs?
**A:** Target is 85% reduction in development costs, 70% in support costs, and 60% shorter sales cycles. ROI is projected at 480% over 3 years.

### Q: What are the biggest risks to the plan?
**A:** Top risks are: market adoption rate, AI agent reliability, team capacity constraints, and revenue shortfall. Each has detailed mitigation plans in Section 3.

### Q: How often should we review the strategic plan?
**A:** Daily standups for critical issues, weekly product sync, monthly strategic review, quarterly board review, and annual comprehensive planning.

### Q: Can we adjust the plan as we learn more?
**A:** Absolutely. The plan is designed for iteration. Use monthly strategic reviews to adjust based on learnings and market feedback.

### Q: Who owns each part of the strategic plan?
**A:** Ownership is defined in each section and sub-issue template. Generally: CEO (overall strategy), CTO (technical/AI), CPO (product), CMO (marketing), CFO (financial).
